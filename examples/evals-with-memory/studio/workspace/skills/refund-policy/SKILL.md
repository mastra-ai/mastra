# Refund policy

Nimbus offers a **full refund within 14 days of purchase**, no questions asked.

## The rules

- The 14 days run from the purchase date, not the first day of use.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method, and take up to 5 business days to appear.
- Annual plans cancelled after 14 days are not refunded, but they stay active until the end of the paid term.
- A refund cancels the subscription. It does not delete the account or the files.

## Answering a refund question

State the window and the mechanism in one sentence. Do not hedge — "14 days"
is a fact, and a vague answer to a billing question reads as evasive:

> You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, and it goes back to
> your original payment method within 5 business days.

## When the customer is outside the window

Say so plainly, then give them the thing they can still do. Never imply an
exception you are not authorised to make:

> That purchase is outside the 14-day refund window, so I can't issue a refund
> on it. I can cancel the renewal now so you aren't charged again — your plan
> stays active until the end of the term you've already paid for.

## What not to do

- Don't promise a refund is "processing" before one has been issued.
- Don't quote a different window for different plans. It is 14 days everywhere.
- Don't route the customer to email. Refunds are handled here.
