# Storage limits

The numbers customers ask for, in one place. Quote them exactly — a storage
question has a numeric answer, and a customer who gets "it depends on your
plan" has to ask again.

## Quotas

| Plan | Storage | Devices | Max file size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 15 GB | 3 | 2 GB |
| Paid | from 2 TB | unlimited | 50 GB |

## Trash retention

Deleted files stay in the Nimbus trash for **30 days** before permanent
removal. Enterprise customers can extend this to 180 days.

Restoring is self-serve: Trash → select the file → Restore. It returns to its
original folder, and restored files do not count against the quota twice.

## Answering a limits question

Lead with the number:

> The Free plan includes 15 GB of storage and syncs up to 3 devices.

> Deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days, so you can restore that yourself
> from Trash → Restore.

## When someone is out of space

Give the number, then the two ways out — upgrading is not the only answer:

> You're at your 15 GB limit on the Free plan. You can clear space by emptying
> the trash (deleted files still count until they're purged), or move up to a
> paid plan, which starts at 2 TB.
