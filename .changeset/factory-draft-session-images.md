You can now attach images when starting a new session in Factory. Paste, drop or pick images on the "What do you want to work on?" screen and they are sent with your first prompt once the session is created. You can also start a session with only an image. Before, the composer showed "Images can be attached once the session is ready." and you had to send a prompt first. If the session can't be created or the first message fails to send, your text and images stay in the composer so you can retry.
