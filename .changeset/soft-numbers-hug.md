Added a thinking level to your personal defaults. In **Settings → Models**, the Personal defaults and Factory defaults cards now pair each model picker with its thinking level, replacing the separate **Thinking** row. Chats on your personal defaults start at your personal level, or at the Factory level when you have not set one. Sessions and work-item runs on the Factory defaults keep the Factory level. The composer thinking control now says which default a session follows: **Personal default** or **Factory default**.
