Added a **New session** button to the top of the Factory sidebar. It opens an empty session draft, the same as the **+** next to Sessions. The search button and the collapse button are gone from the top of the open sidebar: open search with Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux), and collapse the sidebar with its resize handle or `[`. The sidebar toggle still shows in the page header while the sidebar is collapsed.
