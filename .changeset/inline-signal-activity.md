`SignalActivity` and `SkillMessage` take `appearance="inline"` to read like the agent's own activity lines. An inline signal is one line: its icon, a readable name (`factory-environment` reads as "Factory environment"), and the mode in plain words. It opens onto the message as prose, with line breaks kept and never in monospace, and a message short enough to fit stays on the line.
