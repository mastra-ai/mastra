Separated the two clocks the Factory overview was reading on one page.

"9 in flight, 4 cards critical" is true this second; "218 shipped, 32m lead time" is only true against a date range — and the range picker at the top looked like it governed both. The windowed figures stay under the picker; what the board is holding right now moved into its own section at the foot of the page, out from under a control that never applied to it.

The "Waiting to start" readout counts the cards synced from GitHub or Linear that no run was ever started on. Every delivery figure deliberately ignores those, since the integrations mirror far more issues than the Factory works on — which left the standing queue invisible. It sits next to the rate that fills it ("8 of 20 filed in the last 30 days picked up"), because a backlog only means something against the demand behind it.
