The chat composer stays one line until you focus it or start writing, so more of the conversation fits on screen, with the model still in view. Below it, a status footer shows who is steering the session on the left and token speed and memory on the right. The footer gives its row back while it has nothing to show, and opening the composer never moves "+" or Send.

Teammates steering someone else's session no longer see the provider icon twice next to the model: the locked model's tooltip now names whose credential their messages use.
