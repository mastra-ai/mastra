The comments panel on a session now opens at half the height of the chat and grows with the conversation up to the full column, instead of opening full height around an empty feed. It morphs open from the workspace card, the composer is a flush bar at the bottom edge and takes focus as the panel opens, and a comment that lands while you are watching rises into place. Editing a comment now saves on Enter, keeps Shift+Enter for a new line, and carries its Cancel and Save inside the bottom right of the field. The edit field grows with its text up to ten lines and then scrolls, with no resize handle, and the workspace card's corners are now concentric with the rows inside it. Session rows in the sidebar no longer open their hover details on a touch viewport, where the card could only appear behind the tap that already navigated away.

On the board, a sent comment now lands in the feed as soon as the server stores it, with no dimmed placeholder state, and a failure keeps the draft with an error instead. The card's thread moved out of the details column into its own panel that slides out from behind the open card, sitting slightly lower beside it; the details box keeps the card's own width so the seam between the two lands on the next column's edge. The description always shows in full on the panel, the column scrolls it under a sticky header and above the sticky actions, the panel stopping at its own set height rather than growing to the screen, and while it loads a skeleton holds the space so the action button stays put. The board dims under the open panel so the card holds the room's attention, and a click on the dim closes the card without pressing whatever sat underneath. An empty feed says so in one quiet line instead of showing a bare composer. On a phone the sheet now hugs its content instead of opening at a fixed height; a Comments button beside the card's status slides the thread in over the details, with a Details button to slide back, and the sheet glides between the two heights instead of snapping.
