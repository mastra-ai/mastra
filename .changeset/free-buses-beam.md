Polished the Knowledge explorer. Imported scopes show their source, such as Linear, with its icon instead of a raw connection id. Page headers show a version chip and relative timestamps with exact times on hover, and the page's child pages open in place as a tree. Loading states use skeletons shaped like the content, graph and page controls use real tooltips, and search results float over the layout with a scroll fade.
