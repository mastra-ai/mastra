Added text selection in the prompt input box. Shift+arrow keys select characters and lines, Shift+Alt or Shift+Ctrl with ←/→ select by word, and Shift+Home/End select to the line edges; Backspace, typing and pasting then work on the selection like they do after a mouse drag. Keyboard selection works with the Mouse setting off.

With input box mouse mode on, a double click selects the word under the pointer, a triple click selects the line, dragging after a double click grows the selection by whole words, and Shift+click extends the selection to the click. Like a drag, these copy the selected text to the clipboard.
