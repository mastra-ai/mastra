Added board suggestions and an activity summary to the Factory new session page, which now asks "What should we ship?" with your first name.

**Suggestion cards**

Cards under the composer show what waits on your board, such as failed runs, pull requests with changes requested, untriaged Intake cards or stale pull requests, each with a count and a one-click action. Pointing at a card previews its prompt in the composer. Clicking it, or pressing Enter while the prompt shows, starts the session. Prompts that change cards act on five at most.

**Activity summary**

One line totals what came in, how many agent runs started and what shipped this week, today or this month. Clicking it opens a chart of the period, and a recap prompt is offered when something shipped.
