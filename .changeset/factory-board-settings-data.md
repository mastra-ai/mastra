Factory boards can be created, reset and deleted, and their intake rules, intake order, Not routed items, dry runs and tests read and written through `client.getFactory(id)` and new `@mastra/react` hooks (`useCreateBoardMutation`, `useIntakeRouteWrites`, `useIntakeOrderQuery`, `useNotRoutedQuery`, `useIntakeDryRunQuery`, `useIntakeTestQuery`, and others).
