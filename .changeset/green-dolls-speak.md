Factory board cards from Vanta, Dependabot and Detail no longer show as cards filed by hand. Each shows its own mark (the Vanta logo, the GitHub mark for Dependabot alerts, the Detail icon), screen readers announce where the card came from, and its link says where it goes. Organizations that have connected Vanta can also filter the board to Vanta cards.

Cards filed by hand, the **PR opened** notification in a session, the **Item state** rule field and the status row of a Studio experiment result show a ring with a dot again, instead of an icon that looked like a face.
