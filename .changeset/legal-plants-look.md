Added mouse mode for the input box. Press Alt+M to turn it on: click to move the cursor, drag to select text (the selection is copied to your clipboard), and type, paste, or press Backspace to replace or remove the selection. A `mouse` badge shows in the status line while it is on. It turns off when you send a message or press Alt+M again, so the terminal's own scrolling and text selection work as usual the rest of the time. On macOS, Alt+M needs your terminal to treat Option as Meta.
