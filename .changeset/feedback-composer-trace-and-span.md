Added a feedback composer to the Feedback tabs of Studio's observability view. Both the trace panel and the span panel now show a text box above the feedback list where you can write a comment and submit it with the arrow button (or Enter), matching the look of the chat composer. Feedback submitted from the trace panel is recorded against the trace, feedback submitted from a span is recorded against that span, and the list refreshes as soon as the comment is saved.
