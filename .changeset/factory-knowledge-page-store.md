The Knowledge page works again on the page store. It shows the project's Knowledge as a page tree beside either the graph or a full page view (markdown body, child pages, mentions, notes and activity), the same explorer Studio uses. Scope circles are captioned with their declaration's `displayLabel` when one is set, with the address's parameters filled in (`"$orgId policy"` shows as "acme policy"). The tree loads each level as it is opened, so every page is reachable, not just the graph's first 200. The graph also draws access grants between scopes (for example from a role scope to the policy scope it can read), naming the role on hover, for the principal scopes the member can read. Edits agents make show up without a reload: the page follows a new change stream (`GET /web/factory/projects/:id/knowledge/events`) and refreshes as writes land, falling back to a refresh every few seconds when the stream is unavailable. Members see their organization's and the project's pages, never another project's or a session's. The deploying app's Knowledge must declare `project:$projectId` scopes (as Factory session runs already need); without them the page reports a configuration error instead of guessing what to show.
