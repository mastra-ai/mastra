Factory sessions now show whose account pays for the next reply, as an icon right after the model under the composer: a building for the organization, or the payer's avatar for a personal account. Hover or focus it to read "Organization account pays for replies", "Your account pays for replies", or a teammate's name such as "Shane Thomas’s account pays for replies". It shows on a new session too, so you know whose credentials a reply uses before you send the first message.
