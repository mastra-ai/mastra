Chats on personal defaults no longer borrow from the Factory defaults. In **Personal** routing and in **Hybrid** chats, a chat with no personal thinking level now starts at medium instead of the Factory thinking level, and the New session page asks you to choose a personal default model instead of starting on the Factory model. The Factory defaults card in Personal routing now says it is **Not used in this scope**, and the composer labels the medium fallback **Built-in default**.
