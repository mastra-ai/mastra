Improved loading states across the Factory app. Board settings, boards, card drafts, sessions, settings, sign-in and onboarding now show their real headings, labels and buttons while loading, with placeholders only where data is still on its way, so content no longer jumps when it arrives. Settings no longer flash a made-up value such as "Off", "Ask" or "Select a model" before the saved one loads: the field shows a placeholder until the real value arrives. Controls that can't act yet look normal but don't respond until the page has loaded. Each loading area announces one loading message to screen readers instead of empty placeholder sections.
