The Factory sidebar footer now has one user menu instead of separate account and Settings links. It shows your profile picture, name and email, and opens My account, Preferences, Your models, Your memory, Connections, Settings, a Theme choice and Log out. Without sign-in, it opens the same settings without My account or Log out.

- Theme opens System, Light and Dark as a submenu you can reach with the arrow keys.
- Back to app returns to the page where you opened settings, including its filters.
