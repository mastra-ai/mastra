Members invited to a Factory now get a setup page instead of a dead end. When sessions run on your own account (personal or hybrid model routing) and you haven't connected one, opening the Factory takes you to setup once, where you connect your provider and pick a model. A new session that can't start shows the same step inline, with **Start chatting**, instead of **No model available**. Slack's **Connect account** and **Finish setup** cards end on this page, with a way back to your Slack thread, and the Slack settings page now says whether **Sign in with Slack** worked.
