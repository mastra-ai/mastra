You can now annotate parts of an agent's reply in Studio and Factory chat. Select text in a message and click **Add to chat**: the passage gets a numbered comment bubble in the chat that stays with the text as you scroll, and the composer counts what you added (for example **2 annotations**). Click a bubble to comment on that passage or remove it, or open the count to jump to a passage. When you send, the agent receives a numbered list of the passages, each quoted with its comment, ahead of what you typed. Annotations come back if a message fails to send.
