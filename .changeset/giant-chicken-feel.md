Added `CircleDotIcon`, a ring with a dot drawn on the Central Icons grid and stroke, for things that are open or made by hand. Use it where `IconCircleRecord` stood in for a ring with a dot: that icon is a voicemail mark.

```tsx
import { CircleDotIcon } from '@mastra/playground-ui/icons/CircleDotIcon';

<CircleDotIcon aria-hidden />;
```
