Factory review, re-review, test report, triage, and self-review comments are now shorter and lead with what matters. Reviews open with the verdict as a heading, then a one-sentence summary, a ranked findings table (blocking first, with a type for each finding), and the required changes. Supporting notes sit in one collapsed block, and the detailed run record stays in the Factory session instead of the PR.
