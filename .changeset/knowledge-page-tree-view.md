Added `KnowledgePageTree` and `KnowledgePageDetail` to the Knowledge explorer. The tree nests scopes and pages in stored order, opens the path to a page selected elsewhere (graph or search), and supports arrow-key navigation. The detail pane shows a page's home scope, synopsis, body, pin audience and notes, with each note's stamped scopes and author and links for resolved `[[mentions]]`.
