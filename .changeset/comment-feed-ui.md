The work item comment feed is now on the board card and in the session's workspace panel. Opening a card lands on the conversation: the description clamps behind "Show more", the feed scrolls in its own region, and the composer sits under it.

Comments post without waiting for the server, quote a selected passage when you reply, autocomplete @mentions from the project roster, and can be edited or deleted in place. Cards show a comment count, and a comment posted elsewhere appears within a few seconds — carried by the board poll the page already makes, so an open feed adds no requests of its own.
