Sending the first message from a Factory card no longer swaps the page for a new one. The message shows as soon as you send it, and the card's header, stage bar and session panel stay in place while the session starts. The panel shows the card and its pull requests right away instead of loading them again. When the thread opens, the composer keeps its focus and model and no longer replays its entrance.

Fixed the first message from a card sometimes never being sent and landing back in the composer.
