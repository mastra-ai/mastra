'@mastra/factory': patch
---

Boards now update within a second of a change when they haven't refreshed in the last 5 seconds, and at the 5 second mark otherwise, instead of on a fixed 2 to 5 second poll. Moving or editing a card, a decision being proposed, picked up, retried or finished, and a session starting, parking, finishing or being torn down mid-run now reach every open board right away, including sessions picked back up after a server restart. While a board is connected it no longer asks for its cards, decisions and running sessions every 2 to 5 seconds; it checks once a minute as a safety net, and goes back to the old pace if the live connection drops. A live connection that goes silent, for example after a server restart behind a proxy, is now reopened within about a minute instead of staying dead until the page is reloaded, so comments and boards pick up live updates again. On boards whose first column isn't Intake, that column now shows its loading placeholder while new items load.
