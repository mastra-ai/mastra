Improved the trace view of a thread in Studio. The thread page now exposes a single **Show traces** toggle (the slide-in traces panel is gone), long traces are clamped to the height of their message with a **Show more** / **Show less** control, the row currently in view is emphasised as you scroll, opening a tool call highlights the spans behind it (the **Highlight spans** button remains for text-only messages), and **View full thread** from a trace lands you on that trace's row.
