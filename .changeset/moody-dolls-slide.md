Dashboards authored in chat now accept the same widgets as the dashboard editor: KPIs that combine values (ratio, difference, max, fallback), read trace, span or score aggregates (for example p95 span duration) and use duration, percent or score formats; line charts of scores; six more chart colors (blue, orange, pink, yellow, red, purple); and optional KPI icon and detail, series color and table empty message.
