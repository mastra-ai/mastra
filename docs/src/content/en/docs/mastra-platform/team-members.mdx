# Team members

Everyone in a Mastra platform organization can access its projects. Each member has a role that decides what they can change. Manage members on the **Team** page in your organization's settings.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| - | - |
| Admin | Everything a member can do, plus manage members and invitations, billing, alerting configuration, organization settings, and Memory and Knowledge instances. |
| Member | Create and change projects, environments, environment variables, deploys, Gateway settings, integrations, and [access tokens](/docs/mastra-platform/access-tokens). |
| Viewer | Read projects, alerts, dashboards, and saved views. Viewers can't change anything. |

An organization always needs at least one admin. You can't remove the only admin, change their role, or leave the organization as the only admin.

## Invite a member

Only admins can invite members.

1. Open your organization's settings in the [Mastra platform](https://projects.mastra.ai) and select **Team**.
1. Select **Invite member**.
1. Enter the person's email address and choose a role.
1. Select **Send invite**.

The person gets an email with a link to join. The dialog can also show the invite link so you can share it directly. The link works once and expires after 7 days.

Pending invitations appear under **Invitations** until they're accepted. To cancel one, revoke it. The link stops working immediately, and you can send a new invitation later.

## Change a member's role

Admins can change a member's role from the **Members** list. The new role applies to everything in the organization. To change the role of the only admin, first make another member an admin.

## Remove a member

Admins can remove a member from the **Members** list. The member loses access to every resource in the organization immediately. You can invite them again later.

Access tokens a member creates are tied to them and stop working when they leave the organization. Before you remove a member, replace any tokens they created that CI or deployed apps use. Have a member who's staying in the organization create the replacement tokens, so they don't stop working too. Confirm that CI and deployed apps use the new tokens, then remove the member.

## Leave an organization

To leave an organization, open the **Team** page and select **Leave organization**. You lose access to all of its projects and resources. If you're the only admin, assign another admin first.
