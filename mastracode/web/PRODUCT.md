# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and engineering leads operating a Mastra Code Factory. They use the dashboard during active delivery to understand throughput, spot blocked or aging work, and see where agents and automation are contributing.

## Product Purpose

Mastra Code coordinates software work across connected repositories, agent sessions, work and review boards, automation rules, and operational metrics. The metrics surface should make Factory flow legible at a glance, then support focused inspection of bottlenecks and individual work items.

## Brand Personality

Crisp, operational, and trustworthy. The interface should feel precise and calm under sustained use, with a clear information hierarchy and selective semantic color rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid presentation-only executive dashboards, dense terminal-style monitoring consoles, decorative marketing analytics, glass effects, oversized rounded cards, and repetitive grids of visually identical stat cards. Do not encode status through color alone.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the operational story: flow, bottlenecks, and active work before secondary breakdowns.
- Make every visual earn its space by supporting a decision or drill-down.
- Use restrained structure and selective semantic color so exceptions stand out immediately.
- Preserve context between summary metrics, trends, and the underlying work items.
- Prefer familiar, keyboard-accessible product patterns over novel controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast targets, preserve full keyboard access and visible focus, respect reduced-motion preferences, and pair every color-coded state with text, shape, or position so it remains understandable without color.
