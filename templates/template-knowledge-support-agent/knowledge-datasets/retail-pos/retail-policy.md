# Example team support safety

Under the example team policy, ask about pending offline payments before disruptive changes. Avoid signing out, uninstalling, switching location or mode, and factory resetting while any offline payments remain pending. These actions can permanently lose the payments. Advise only. Never collect card data or credentials. Obtain customer authorization before any disruptive change. If evidence is insufficient, ask a targeted question or escalate. Confirm observations before recording a resolution.
