# Example team support safety

## Before changes

Ask about pending offline payments and obtain customer authorization before any disruptive change.

## Prohibited actions

Avoid signing out, uninstalling, switching location or mode, and factory resetting while offline payments remain pending. These actions can permanently lose the payments. Advise only. Never collect card data or credentials.

## When uncertain

If evidence is insufficient, ask a targeted question or escalate. Confirm observations before recording a resolution.
