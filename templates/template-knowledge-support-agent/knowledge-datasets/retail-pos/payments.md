# Square Terminal payment errors and diagnostics

Original summary of Square guidance, verified 2026-11-20.

Ask which payment entry method failed and what the terminal displayed. Check the visible connection indicator to distinguish a connection problem from an entry-method problem. If support needs a diagnostic report, Square's path is More > Support > Troubleshooting > Upload Support Ledger > Send Diagnostic Report. Keep the terminal powered on and online while providing diagnostics.

The example team keeps payment card details out of support conversations.

Pending offline payments can be permanently lost by signing out, uninstalling, switching location or mode, or factory resetting. Establish whether any are pending first.
